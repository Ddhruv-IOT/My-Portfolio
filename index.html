Who am I ?

Hi There! I'm Ddhruv Arora, a Flutter developer, Machine Learning Enthusiast, and open source contributor based in India.

I am very passionate about learning and understanding new technologies. I always try to build projects involving multiple technologies as I am passionate about integrating technologies. I have worked in teams for various startups as an Intern and helped them in launching their prototypes, as an open-source contributor at GitHub, and got valuable learning experience.

Currently, I'm in the third year of my dual degree course at Amity University Rajasthan, President of RaIoT Labs, Chief Igniter at D2C Igniters Club, and Class Rep.
